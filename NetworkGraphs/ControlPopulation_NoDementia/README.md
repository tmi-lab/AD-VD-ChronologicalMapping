Control Cohort - matched on sex and age at diagnosis of dementia subtype. 

This repository contains the Network Graphs generated for each timeframe and group related to the matched control populations, as described in the accompanying manuscript.

These network graphs were produced to visually represent the comorbidity patterns identified in the study, offering insights into how various conditions interact over time in populations who were matched to dementia individuals on age at diagnosis and sex, but who themselves do not have a diagnosis of dementia sub-type. 

Full methodological details, including how the networks were constructed and analyzed, can be found in the manuscript.

Each file is labelled according to the matched individual with dementia's sub-type and time frame it refers to.
